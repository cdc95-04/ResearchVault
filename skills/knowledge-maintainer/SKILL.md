# Knowledge maintainer

Read [evidence](../../references/evidence.md), then only the template matching the
requested page: [concept](../../templates/concept.md),
[method](../../templates/method.md), [relation](../../templates/relation.md),
[controversy](../../templates/controversy.md),
[benchmark](../../templates/benchmark.md) or [theme](../../templates/theme.md).

Inspect existing Knowledge and the actual linked Notes before adding a page.
Combine compatible claims while retaining distinct tasks, bodies, metrics and
assumptions. Explain conflicting evidence instead of averaging it into consensus.
Method pages own procedures and assumptions; relation pages own typed connections;
controversies own competing explanations; benchmarks own evaluation conditions.

Route important claims through a Note to the parent, Fulltext and attachment.
Do not cite a Knowledge page as primary evidence or promote a metadata-only
candidate into a read source. Use interpretation or speculative labels where the
synthesis extends beyond the observations.

Show a focused diff for existing pages and preserve stable knowledge_id values.
Ordinary Knowledge does not require a provenance sidecar. Deliver what changed,
the supporting Notes and unresolved conflicts; Research objects belong to
research-maintainer.

# Research maintainer

Read [science](../../references/science.md). Load only the needed template:
[question](../../templates/question.md), [gap](../../templates/gap.md),
[hypothesis](../../templates/hypothesis.md), [idea](../../templates/idea.md),
[card](../../templates/card.md) or [project](../../templates/project.md).
Read [review](../../references/review.md) when assessing a Gap's review gate.

Keep the motivating question, evidence-bounded gap, falsifiable hypothesis,
candidate idea and proposed claim separate. A project indexes those objects; it
does not duplicate their scientific content. Preserve identifiers across edits.
Ideas may be narrowed, merged, abandoned or returned for more literature.

Do not require a gap to exist. An externally_checked Gap needs executed distinct
query families, deduplication, challenge work and declared coverage limits tied
to a frozen corpus. High confidence also needs strong inspected evidence.

A Claim Lock freezes proposition, scope, comparisons, mechanism, falsifiers and
evidence obligations. Preview the canonical snapshot and proposed metadata before
applying the receipt. Revisions identify an existing predecessor and reason;
post-result changes are exploratory. Preserve the claim's history even if a page
link was removed. A digest neither establishes truth nor authorizes execution.

Hand a sufficiently specified locked Card to experiment-manager. Do not invent
experiment results or write a Design Lock as part of claim development.

# Paper Note writer

Read [evidence](../../references/evidence.md), the
[paper template](../../templates/paper.md) and, for substantive robotics reading,
[reading checks](../../references/reading.md). Do not load review or experiment
contracts for an ordinary single-paper note.

Resolve the actual parent, attachment and Fulltext; state which source layers were
inspected. Analyze the problem, claims, system, methods, baselines, results and
limits at the depth supported by the source. Keep authors' claims separate from
observations and your interpretation. Use the seventeen checks to expose missing
evidence, not to fill empty sections with plausible statements.

For a mechanism claim, map the intervention and predicted intermediate effect to
discriminating observations. Performance does not automatically establish body
computation, representation learning or a causal explanation. Capture robotics
timing, embodiment, adaptation and transfer conditions when relevant.

Every important quote, number or conclusion needs a real locator and appropriate
evidence label. Preserve null results, contradictions, denominators and missing
artifacts. A partial Note is acceptable and must say what remains unread.

Reuse exact identity and show proposed changes for an existing Note. Return the
Note plus unresolved evidence needs. Send cross-paper synthesis to
knowledge-maintainer; do not create a research gap from one paper alone.

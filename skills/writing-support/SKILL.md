# Writing support

Read [science](../../references/science.md) and the
[ledger](../../templates/ledger.md). Use the
[claim](../../templates/claim.json) only when a separate literature claim record is
useful; read [evidence](../../references/evidence.md) for such literature claims.

For each consequential statement, identify claim_id, precise result/literature
references, verdict and limits across robot, morphology, task, environment,
seeds, replication, OOD and transfer. Bound wording to those limits. Keep
mechanistic explanations distinguishable from performance observations.

Map repeated numeric quantities explicitly across abstract, body, table,
caption and ledger, retaining units and denominators. Compare equivalent values
and report disagreement without selecting a convenient winner. Do not repair
the evidence merely to match the manuscript.

Preserve NOT_SUPPORTED and INCONCLUSIVE judgments and explain missing evidence.
A speculative sentence cannot become an established finding through stronger
wording. Show proposed edits to existing writing and ledger records together so
the user can assess the change. Deliver grounded wording, the claim map and
unresolved numerical/evidence conflicts; do not invent citations or results.

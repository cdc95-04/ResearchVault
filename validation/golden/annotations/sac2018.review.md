# SAC 2018 independent annotation review

Status: `agent_independent_pending_human`. **No human has reviewed or approved these annotations.** The agent's source checks below are not human verification.

The annotation was authored from the [PMLR publication record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/haarnoja18b.html) and its [main PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/haarnoja18b/haarnoja18b.pdf). The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290) was used only to cross-check bibliographic identity. The annotation's hash identifies the actual downloaded PDF bytes; local copies and page renders remain under ignored `.qa/phase2/papers/`.

## Independence and evidence checks

- No ResearchVault implementation source, tests, existing generated notes, retrieval output, or program-produced claims were read.
- `pypdf` extracted the source pages. Poppler rendered PDF pages 5 and 7, which were visually inspected for formula, algorithm, experiment, and section placement.
- Three paraphrased claims have explicit one-based PDF locators. No article full text or original prose quotations are included in the committed annotation files.
- Both retrieval queries were specified solely from the original source before viewing any implementation behavior. Their expected claim IDs are relevance judgments, not observed retrieval results.
- Neutral blind-capture tasks were placed in ignored scratch storage before the parent read the annotations. They contain only question text and claim IDs, with no answers.
- No actual Zotero library or user vault was accessed. No git commit was made.

## Limitations and identity cautions

The available publisher record does not provide a publisher article DOI. The arXiv DOI is separately labeled. The arXiv and publisher PDFs were not compared byte for byte, and all claim locators belong to the publisher main PDF. Supplemental material was not needed for the selected claims and was not read.

The comparative-evaluation locator refers to the protocol paragraph; the associated plotted figure appears on the preceding PDF page. Numerical facts were taken from explicit prose, not estimated from plotted curves. The label `reviewed_on` records the agent source-check date only.

# Review Protocol

## Question and coverage dimensions

Define the scope before screening and record relevant time, task, morphology,
method and evidence-quality dimensions.

## Search and screening plan

Specify distinct query families, source choices, seed identities, bounded
citation rounds and decision reasons. A fixed paper count is not completion.

## Challenge plan

Search supporting, competing, contradictory, null and alternative-mechanism
work. Preserve the exact queries and actual execution records in the corpus.

## Freeze and limitations

Explain the stopping basis, outstanding areas and revision rule. A freeze binds
the protocol and corpus; it does not certify exhaustive coverage.
